# Kids and Family Safety Notes

These are design and content principles that must be respected in all versions of Arkphone Kids.

---

## Design principles for family use under stress

- **Large touch targets.** All interactive elements must be at least 48 × 48 px.
- **Simple wording.** Use plain language. Avoid technical terms, jargon, and abbreviations without explanation.
- **No scary hype.** Cards should be calm and action-focused. Avoid alarming language beyond what is necessary for safety.
- **Dark mode always.** High-contrast dark UI reduces eye strain in low-light emergencies.
- **Family Mode is for children and non-technical readers.** It simplifies card wording without weakening safety meaning.
- **No clutter.** The most critical information (danger, first 5 actions) appears immediately without scrolling.
- **Obvious categories.** Icons and short labels must identify each scenario at a glance.

---

## What Family Mode must and must not do

**Must:**
- Use shorter sentences.
- Replace technical terms with plain equivalents (e.g. "grown-up" instead of "adult", "turns blue" instead of "cyanosis").
- Keep all safety-critical actions intact.
- Keep all "do not" warnings intact.

**Must not:**
- Omit any step from the first-5 actions.
- Remove any "call emergency services" instruction.
- Change the meaning of danger warnings.
- Replace specific actions with vague reassurance.

---

## Content that is never appropriate for this app

- Step-by-step medical treatment (CPR technique details, wound suturing, medication dosing).
- Electrical repair or wiring instructions.
- Water purification chemistry or improvised disinfection formulas.
- Legal advice.
- Information about using weapons or force.
- Content that could cause panic rather than calm action.

---

## Age-appropriate framing

Arkphone Kids is designed for households with children. Assume:
- A child aged 8–12 may read the Family Mode text without an adult present.
- A stressed adult may be reading the standard text in a hurry.
- Neither reader has professional emergency training.

Frame all content as: "Here is what to do right now. Here is who to call. Here is what to wait for."

---

## Review responsibility

Every card that changes must be reviewed by a human before deployment.
The app's global warning banner must never be removed.
